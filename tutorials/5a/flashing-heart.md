# Flashing heart

## Introduction @unplugged

Let's make a heart flash on the LED display!

## Step 1

Drag a ``||basic:show icon||`` block into ``||basic:on start||`` and choose the heart icon.

```blocks
basic.showIcon(IconNames.Heart)
```

## Step 2

Add a second ``||basic:show icon||`` block and choose the small heart.

```blocks
basic.showIcon(IconNames.Heart)
basic.showIcon(IconNames.SmallHeart)
```

## Step 3

Click ``|Download|`` to copy your program to the micro:bit. Watch the heart flash!
